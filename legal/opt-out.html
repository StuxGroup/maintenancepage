Opt-Out Preferences

The short version: there's nothing to opt out of.

No data sale or sharing

Laws like California's CCPA/CPRA give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. This page has nothing to opt out of in that sense — it doesn't collect personal data for advertising or analytics, so there's none to sell or share.

What actually happens

The only outside data flow is your own browser loading a font and some icons directly from their CDNs, and GitHub Pages serving and logging the page itself (see the Privacy Policy). Neither is something Stux.Group could sell even if it wanted to — we're not in the middle of either request.

Do Not Track

Because this page runs no tracking scripts, a browser's "Do Not Track" signal has no additional effect here — there's nothing being tracked either way.
