Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2026 — applies to this maintenance page (maintenancepage.stux.group).

The short version

This page is a temporary holding page shown while a Stux.Group project or service is offline for maintenance. It's static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages — there's no server-side code of ours behind it, no database, and nothing here designed to collect information about you.

What this page collects

Nothing, directly. There's no account system, no forms, no analytics scripts, and no advertising or tracking pixels anywhere on this page.

Third-party requests

Your browser loads a couple of things directly from third-party CDNs, which may log the request under their own policies:

  • The Barlow font, from Google Fonts.
  • Font Awesome icons, from cdnjs/Cloudflare.

This page itself is served by GitHub Pages, which may log visits per GitHub's own privacy statement. Stux.Group has no visibility into those logs.

localStorage

The only thing this page stores in your browser is a single flag, sg-theme, set when you toggle light/dark mode so your preference is remembered on your next visit. It stays on your device and can be cleared any time via your browser's site data settings.

Social links

The Bluesky and LinkedIn icons on the maintenance page are plain links, not embedded widgets — clicking one takes you to that platform's own site, under its own privacy policy. Nothing is loaded from either platform unless you click through.

Children's privacy

This page isn't directed at children, though since nothing is collected here anyway, that distinction doesn't change anything in practice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, the effective date above will be updated.
