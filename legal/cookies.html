Cookies Policy

Effective: September 2026 — applies to this maintenance page (maintenancepage.stux.group).

No tracking cookies

This page sets no cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics of any kind. There are no third-party ad networks, no analytics scripts, and no tracking pixels here.

What we actually store

The only browser storage this page uses is localStorage — distinct from a cookie, and never sent to any server the way a cookie is. It holds exactly one thing:

  • sg-theme — remembers whether you last chose light or dark mode, so the page opens in that mode next time.

That value stays entirely on your device and can be cleared any time via your browser's site data settings — clearing it just resets the page to its default theme.

GitHub Pages

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may set its own cookies or perform its own logging as part of serving the page, outside anything Stux.Group controls — see GitHub's privacy statement for that.

Consent management
